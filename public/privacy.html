Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains what information Lazy Cub Life ("we", "us") collects when you play at lazycub.life, how it is used, and the choices you have. If you have a question, message @lazyinuit on X.

Information you give us

  • Account: a username and password. We do not ask for your email address, real name or phone number. Passwords are stored securely by our account provider and are never visible to us.
  • ETH wallet address (optional): if you choose to add a public Ethereum wallet address, we store it so we can send you any rewards you earn. We never ask for, and you should never share, private keys or recovery phrases.
  • Profile photo (optional): if you add a photo, it is stored and can be seen by other players who visit your cub or view your profile.

Information created when you play

  • Your cub's save: its needs, level and XP, outfit, unlocked traits, best scores and daily challenge progress.
  • Your Cub Cash balance and a history of what you earned and spent, to keep balances fair and fix mistakes.
  • Your game scores, shown with your username on the public leaderboards, and the weekly prize winner's username.
  • How long you spend in each part of the game, which helps us see which games are enjoyed and keep things fair.

If you play the demo version, your cub is kept only on your device and nothing is sent to us.

Information stored on your device

The game uses your browser's local storage to keep your cub, your settings and your sign-in session on your device, so the game works and you stay signed in. These are needed for the game to work and are not used for advertising.

Advertising and cookies

We may show ads provided by Google AdSense. Google and its partners are third-party vendors that use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.
  • To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and How Google uses cookies in advertising.
  • If you are in the UK, the European Economic Area or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before personalised ads or non-essential cookies are used, through a consent message provided by Google. You can change your choice at any time from that message.

We do not use any other analytics or tracking tools.

How we use information

  • To run the game: save your cub, keep you signed in and show your progress on any device.
  • To run the leaderboards, daily challenges, weekly prizes and Cub Cash.
  • To send rewards to a wallet address you have chosen to give us.
  • To keep the game fair and safe, including spotting cheating and suspending accounts that break the rules.
  • To show ads that help keep the game free.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase stores accounts, cub saves, scores, Cub Cash and profile photos on our behalf.
  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Google provides ads and the consent message, as described above.
  • Other players can see your username, scores, cub, outfit and profile photo.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information for as long as your account exists. If you ask us to delete your account, we will remove your account, cub, wallet address and photo. Some records, such as past leaderboard results and the Cub Cash history, may be kept for a short time to keep balances and prizes fair.

Children

Lazy Cub Life is a family-friendly game that players of all ages may enjoy, but it is not made for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Younger players should only create an account with a parent's or guardian's permission. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the UK and the European Union, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to object to or limit how it is used, and to complain to a data protection authority. To make a request, message @lazyinuit on X with your username.

Keeping information safe

Information is sent over secure connections and stored with access controls. No online service can be completely secure, so please use a password you do not use anywhere else.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
