How to Play

Everything you need to know to look after your cub, earn Cub Cash and climb the leaderboards.

Play Lazy Cub Life

Getting started

Open the doors and choose how to play. Play demo version lets you try everything straight away with demo coins, but nothing is saved. Create an account with just a username and password to keep your cub, earn real Cub Cash and appear on the leaderboards. No email is needed.

Looking after your cub

Your cub's needs are shown on the buttons at the bottom of its room. Keep them topped up to keep your cub happy.

  • Mood: slowly drops over time. Pet your cub to cheer it up. Finishing a mini-game makes it happier too.
  • Energy: each mini-game costs energy. Put your cub to sleep to recover it, or buy Milk or Juice in the shop with Cub Cash for a quick boost.
  • Cleanliness: drops over time and as your cub plays. Wash your cub when it gets muddy.

Caring for your cub and playing games earns XP. Every level slows down how fast your cub's needs drop, so a high level cub is easier to look after.

The wardrobe

Try on any trait for free to see how it looks: bodies, manes, eyes, mouths, headgear, bodygear and earrings, for both young and older cubs. Unlock the ones you want to keep with Cub Cash, then switch them on to dress your cub. Rarer traits cost more and give extra XP when you unlock them.

The mini-games

Choose from seven mini-games, from floating up a jungle river to shooting hoops in a packed stadium. Every game is quick to learn: each has a Rules screen explaining how to play and score before you start. Your score earns XP, lifts your cub's mood and counts towards your daily challenges and the leaderboards, and many games let you collect Cub Cash as you play.

Cub Cash

Cub Cash is the game's coin. Earn it in the mini-games, from daily challenges and from weekly prizes, then spend it in the wardrobe and the shop. Cub Cash has no real-money value.

Daily challenges

Every day there is a challenge for each mini-game, such as reaching a target score. Scores from all your plays that day add up. Each challenge you complete pays 10 Cub Cash, and completing three or more unlocks a bonus dice roll that can win extra Cub Cash or a new wardrobe trait.

Leaderboards and weekly prizes

Every mini-game has weekly and all-time leaderboards, plus an overall board that adds up your best score in each game. At the end of each week the top three players on the overall board win 500, 200 and 100 Cub Cash.

Visiting other cubs

Tap a player on the leaderboard to see their profile, then visit their cub to see its outfit and badges.
