About Lazy Cub Life

Lazy Cub Life is a free game where you look after your very own lion cub, inspired by the Lazy Lions community. It plays in your web browser on phones, tablets and computers, and can be added to your home screen like an app.

Play Lazy Cub Life

Look after your cub

Your cub lives in its own room. Feed it, wash it, let it sleep and give it a pet to keep it happy. Look after it and play with it to earn XP: as your cub levels up, its needs drop more slowly.

Dress it up

Visit the wardrobe to try on hundreds of traits: bodies, manes, eyes, mouths, headgear, bodygear and earrings, for both young and older cubs. Unlock the ones you love with Cub Cash and show your cub off to other players.

Play the mini-games

  • Lazy River: float up a jungle river on your floaties while a wave chases you down.
  • Bubble Blast: pop matching bubbles before they reach the bottom.
  • Kovu's Space Dash: tap to fly through the gaps in space.
  • Lazy Hoops: swipe to shoot baskets in a packed stadium.
  • Ninja Cub Dojo: slice through targets and dodge the bombs.
  • Cupid Archery: pull back and aim for the bullseye.
  • Pride Pairs: match pairs of cub cards from memory.

Earn Cub Cash and climb the leaderboards

Collect Cub Cash in the mini-games and from daily challenges, then spend it in the wardrobe and the shop. Every game has weekly and all-time leaderboards, and the top player on the overall board each week wins a Cub Cash prize.

Try it first

You can play the demo version straight away without an account. Create a free account with just a username and password to save your cub, earn Cub Cash and appear on the leaderboards. No email address is needed.
